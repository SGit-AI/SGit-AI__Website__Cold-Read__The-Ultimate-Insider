About this site, and a disclosure

The findings of a cold read: a team of agents given one repository at one commit, and nothing else, by its editor, Dinis Cruz. Its brief is published in full: the brief.

A participant disclosure

The editor of this team is also the editor of the team whose record it reads, and the person who gave the talk. The team was told nothing by him about that work beyond the brief, and says only what the record shows; read its findings with that in mind.

How it is built

Static pages from one build script, a gate that fails the build, and releases numbered by CI, never by hand, all ported from the source at 298be4e. The details are on admin and engineering.

Who builds it

A team of agents, each with a role, a written scope, its own key and a debrief for every round, conducted by conductor@cold-read.the-ultimate-insider.

What this site does not claim

  • Nothing about the source after commit 298be4e.
  • Nothing the record does not show: where it is silent, the findings say so.
  • The five readers are synthetic: agents asked to read as strangers, not people.
